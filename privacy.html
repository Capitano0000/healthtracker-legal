Privacy Policy

Health Tracker: Daily Diary

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Health Tracker is a personal health and wellbeing journal. Information you enter, including wellbeing scores, symptoms, medication records and personal notes, is stored locally on your device.

We do not operate a backend, require an account, display advertising, track users or collect analytics. Your journal records are not transmitted to us or to external AI services. The app does not use HealthKit or CloudKit.

2. Data Security

Your journal uses iOS file protection. The app marks journal files for exclusion from automatic device backups. iOS manages device backups and may include files even if the app marks them for exclusion.

We recommend keeping your device passcode enabled and protecting access to your device.

3. Backup and Restore

You may manually export a password-protected, encrypted backup of your journal. Backup files are encrypted before export.

The app uses AES-256-GCM encryption to protect exported backups. We cannot access your backup password or recover it if it is lost.

You choose where to store exported files through the system file picker. Your selected storage provider, which may include a cloud storage service, controls its own storage and retention practices.

Restore adds missing records. Imported medication reminders stay off and symptom reminder dates are cleared.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your journal records remain on your device until you remove them or delete the app.

You can edit supported records, delete individual entries or use the Delete All Health Data option in Settings to remove local journal records and cancel reminders.

Deleting a medication preserves its previous dose history.

Deleting local journal data does not remove previously exported backup files or support correspondence. These must be managed separately.

5. Notifications

The app uses optional local notifications to provide reminders. Notification permissions, display and delivery are controlled by iOS.

Medication names are hidden in notifications by default. If you choose to display medication names, this information may become visible to others who can see your notifications.

6. Support Communications

If you voluntarily contact support, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message. The app does not automatically attach your journal records.

We use your email address and message to answer support requests. Do not send medical records. You can request deletion by email.

7. Advertising and Tracking

Health Tracker does not contain advertising or tracking services. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Your Privacy Rights

You control the information stored in your journal and can remove it through the app's Settings.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you have voluntarily provided through support communications, subject to applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or applicable legal requirements. The current version will be available on this page.
