Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my journal stored?

Your journal records are stored locally on your device. No account is required.

Can I delete my information?

Yes. You can delete individual records or use Delete All Health Data in the app's Settings.

Can I back up my journal?

Yes. The app supports manual encrypted backups protected by a password you choose. Lost backup passwords cannot be recovered.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check your iOS notification permissions and Focus settings. Notification delivery is controlled by iOS.

Does Health Tracker provide medical advice?

No. Health Tracker is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Medical Disclaimer

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making medical decisions. Do not rely on this app as your only reminder for essential medication or emergencies.
