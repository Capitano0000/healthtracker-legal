1. Purpose of the App

Health Tracker is intended for personal record-keeping and organization. It helps you record your wellbeing, symptoms, medications and other personal information.

Health Tracker helps you organize information you enter yourself. You remain responsible for the accuracy of your entries.

2. Medical Disclaimer

Health Tracker is intended for personal record-keeping and organization. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and does not replace a healthcare professional. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before making medical decisions.

Scores are subjective and entered by you. The app does not interpret symptoms, check medication doses or recommend changing treatment. Unconfirmed does not mean missed. Never take an extra dose based on this app.

3. Reminders

Local reminders can be delayed or suppressed by iOS, Focus, permissions or device state. Do not use this app as your only reminder for essential medication or for emergencies.

4. Device-Only Journal

Your journal stays on this device. Deleting the app, losing this device, or replacing it may permanently erase your records.
