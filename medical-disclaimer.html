Medical Disclaimer

Health Tracker is intended for personal record-keeping and organization. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and does not replace a healthcare professional. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before making medical decisions.

Scores and condition statuses are subjective and entered by you. The app does not interpret symptoms, check medication doses or recommend changing treatment. Unconfirmed does not mean missed. Never take an extra dose based on this app.

Local reminders can be delayed or suppressed by iOS, Focus, permissions or device state. Do not use this app as your only reminder for essential medication or for emergencies.
